## 2.2.7

- **New text editing screen with live preview**: when you edit a sign, the sign itself is shown
  head-on and you type directly on it (with a blinking cursor), just like vanilla signs. The text is
  drawn exactly as it will look in the world.
- Each sign is framed and scaled to fit the screen, including the wide **station name sign**.
- Signs with several texts (double/triple speed, double platform number, hectometre post and the
  two-line warning sign) are now edited **all in the same screen**: use the Up/Down arrows or Tab to switch
  field, Enter to go to the next one / confirm, Esc to cancel.
