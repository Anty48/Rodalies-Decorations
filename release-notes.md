## 2.3.1

### New block
- **Quartz with Top Red Line**: a red stripe along the top edge of the side faces. Recipe: red dye
  over a quartz block gives the **one-sided** version (1). It converts 1:1 into the version with the
  stripe on every side, and back, like the other stripes.

### Recipe changes
- **Quartz with Red Line** is now red dye **next to** a quartz block, side by side (either side).
  Red dye over quartz now makes the new top red line.
- **Quartz block and smooth quartz are interchangeable**: every recipe that uses one of them now
  accepts the other (Cercanias logo, Renfe logo, purple stripe, all the stripes, train window,
  white train door and train maximum speed sign).

Both Forge 1.20.1 and NeoForge 1.21.1 get the same changes.
