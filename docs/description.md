# Rodalies Decorations

Rodalies Decorations adds decorative blocks and railway signage based on the Rodalies de Catalunya
and Renfe networks. It is intended for building stations, platforms, lineside equipment and rolling
stock. The mod does not change gameplay or world generation: every block is decorative, with a few
interactions for configuring signs and doors.

## Content

**Track signalling**
- Speed limit, temporary speed restriction (TSR) and end of TSR, warning sign, halt, whistle and
  immediate stop signals.
- Each signal is available in a full-height and a short version, and can be mounted on walls and
  thin supports such as fences, walls or catenary masts.
- Speed signals can be stacked into double and triple assemblies, or combined with an end-of-TSR sign.
- Hectometre posts.

**Station signage**
- Rodalies station sign, generic station sign and platform-number sign, all with editable text.

**Logos and finishes**
- Rodalies "R", Renfe and Cercanías logos, and quartz with the purple stripe, each with a one-sided
  variant.
- Orange and purple stripes (centred, bottom and double over grey concrete).

**Rolling stock**
- Class 447 windshield, train window, train lights and front lights.
- Sliding train doors in orange and white, opening forwards or backwards.
- Train maximum-speed sign.

## Editing text

Signs with text are edited on a preview of the sign itself: the text is typed directly onto the
sign and shown exactly as it will appear in the world. Signs with several fields (stacked speed
signals, double platform numbers, hectometre posts) are edited in a single screen.

## Compatibility

- Create 6.0 or newer (optional, Forge 1.20.1 only): sliding doors open automatically when a Create
  train stops at a station. Without Create, or with an older version, the doors are opened by hand.
- Every block has a crafting recipe; see the wiki for the full list.
